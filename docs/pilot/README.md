# Pilot of Chapters 1, 3 and 10

Status: materials prepared on 2026-10-22 and updated on 2026-10-30 for the complete first draft; participants not yet recruited.

Step 5 of the [implementation sequence](../plan.md#implementation-sequence) is to pilot Chapters 1, 3 and 10 and their exercises with a small group of practising evidence-synthesis librarians, and to record where they fail. This folder holds what the pilot needs.

| File | For | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| [`protocol.md`](protocol.md) | The author | Aims, participants, procedure, what is recorded and how decisions follow from it |
| [`invitation.md`](invitation.md) | The author, to personalise and send | A draft invitation: what is asked, who is eligible, how feedback is used |
| [`participant-guide.md`](participant-guide.md) | Participants | What to read, how to attempt the exercises, how to give feedback, time needed |
| [`facilitator-guide.md`](facilitator-guide.md) | The author or a facilitator | The knowledge check and its answers, the debrief questions, how to log findings |
| [`findings.md`](findings.md) | The author | The log of what participants found, with the decision taken on each item |

Feedback reaches the repository through two GitHub issue forms in `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/`: **Pilot feedback** (one per chapter) and **Error report** (for anything wrong in the book, from anyone). The forms work once they are on the `main` branch.

## Why these three chapters

Chapter 1 defines the problem and the vocabulary; Chapter 10 carries the book's central argument; Chapter 3 is the largest gap in existing teaching and the first test of how much retrieval mechanism readers want. The plan defers decisions about the depth of Part II (Chapters 2 to 6) until the pilot shows what readers needed, so Chapter 3 stands in for that part.

Every chapter is now drafted, Chapters 5 and 6 at a provisional depth. Participants may read any of them, but the pilot asks only about 1, 3 and 10, so that its questions stay answerable in the time asked; Chapter 3's depth rating decides the depth of Chapters 5 and 6.
